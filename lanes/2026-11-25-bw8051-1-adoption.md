## Qualified behavior and boundaries

The hourglass proves the unchanged early driven/released LED scan; the unselected line remains dark. The clock proves common-anode `12:34` segment currents, main-loop progress at both ADC endpoints, and actual DS1302 rollover from `12:59:59` to `13:00:00` reaching the guest buffer. Both use W408AS at 11.0592 MHz and 5 V. Source patches and licenses accompany the downloadable archive.

Later hourglass tilt/sleep stages, complete clock menus/alarms, NMEA, IAP/EEPROM persistence, exact STC15 ADC latency/interrupts, Timer2 reload/interrupts, watchdog CPU reset, full UART timing and pin-level SPI remain outside this qualification. Details are in `docs/BW8051-ADOPTION.md` and `docs/BW8051-TEACHING.md`.
